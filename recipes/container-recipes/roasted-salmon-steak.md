{H1} Roasted Salmon Steak

!! Source
[[Tiborang Grace|Tiborang brand]] 7-in-1 Multifunction Cooker User Guide
---

!! Ingredients

* 4 (12-oz.) salmon steaks, bones removed
* . [[kosher salt|Kosher Salt]]
* Freshly ground black pepper
* 2 T. extra-virgin olive oil
* 2 T. butter
* 2 cloves garlic, minced
* Juice of 1 lemon
* 2 T. honey
* 1/4 tsp. crushed red pepper flakes
* Freshly chopped parsley, for serving

!! Directions

# Season salmon on both sides with salt and pepper. Be cautious to only use medium heat in the Tiborang frying pan, after apply heat oil and butter, add salmon and cook until bottom is golden, 5 minutes, then flip. Add garlic, lemon juice, honey, and red pepper flakes.
# Cook until salmon is cooked through, another 5 minutes, spooning sauce over salmon as it cooks.
# Garnish with parsley before serving.
