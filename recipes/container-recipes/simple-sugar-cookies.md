{H1} Simple Sugar Cookies

!! Source
[[Baker's Corner|Aldi's brand]] Granulated Sugar
---

!! Ingredients

* 1 c. [[Granulated Sugar|Granulated Sugar]]
* 1 c. Butter or . [[margarine|Margarine]], softened
* 1 [[egg|Egg]]
* 1 T. Vanilla extract
* 2-1/4 c. [[all-purpose flour|All-Purpose Flour]]
* 1 tsp. [[baking soda|Baking Soda]]

!! Directions

# In a large bowl, mix sugar and butter. Beat in egg and vanilla until light and fluffy. Mix in flour and baking soda. 
# Divide dough into halves. Shape each half into a roll about 1-1/2 in. in diameter. Wrap each half in wax paper and refrigerate for approximately 1 hour until chilled. 
# Preheat oven to 375°F. Unwrap rolls and cut into 1/4-in. slices. Sprinkle each slice with a pinch of sugar. Place on ungreased pan and bake for 10-12 minutes or until lightly browned around the edges. 
# Cool on wire rack. Store in an airtight container once the cookies have cooled.
