{H1} Nestle Toll House Famous Fudge

!! Source
[[Nestle Toll House|Nestle brand]] Semi-Sweet Chocolate Morsels chip bag
---
''Makes'': 24 2-piece servings

!! Ingredients

* 1-1/2 c. [[granulated sugar|Granulated Sugar]]
* 2/3 c. (5 fl.-oz. can) Nestle Carnation Evaporated Milk
* 2 T. butter
* 1/4 tsp. [[salt|Salt]]
* 2 c. miniature marshmallows
* 1-1/2 c. (9-0z.) Nestle Toll House Semi-Sweet Chocolate Morsels
* 1/2 c. chopped [[pecans|Pecans]] or [[walnuts|Walnuts]] (optional)
* 1 tsp. vanilla extract

!! Directions

# Line 8-inch-square baking pan with foil.
# In a medium, heavy-duty saucepan, combine sugar, evaporated milk, butter and salt.
# Over medium heat, bring to a full rolling boil, stirring constantly. Continue to boil, stirring constantly, for 4 to 5 minutes.
# Remove from heat.
# Stir in marshmallows, morsels, nuts and vanilla extract all at once. Stir vigorously for 1 minute or until marshmallows are melted.
# Immediately pour into prepared baking pan. Refrigerate for 2 hours or until firm.
# Lift from pan; remove foil. Cut into 48 pieces.
