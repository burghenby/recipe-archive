{H1} Tandoori Chicken Wrap

!! Source
[[Tiborang Grace|Tiborang brand]] 7-in-1 Multifunction Cooker User Guide
---

!! Ingredients

* 1-3/4 c. [[yogurt|Plain Yogurt]] (whole-milk or low-fat)
* 2-1/2 T. [[minced ginger|Minced Ginger]]
* 1-1/2 T. [[minced garlic|Minced Garlic]]
* 1 T. crushed red chile flakes, or to taste
* 1-1/4 T. [[ground cumin|Ground Cumin]]
* 1-1/4 T. dried oregano
* 1 T. [[table salt|Table Salt]]
* 1/2 T. freshly ground black pepper
* 3 T. olive oil
* 1-1/2 lb. boneless, skinless chicken breasts
* Naan, pita, or flour tortilla, warmed
* Romaine or leafy lettuce (optional)
* 1-1/2 c. [[yogurt|Plain Yogurt]] (preferably whole-milk)
* 1/2 T. [[ground cumin|Ground Cumin]]
* 3 T. chopped mint
* 1 T. [[table salt|Table Salt]], or to taste

!! Directions

# In a large bowl, combine all ingredients. [Note: 1-3/4 c. [[yogurt|Yogurt]], 2-1/2 T. ginger, 1-1/2 T. garlic, 1 T. red chile flakes, 1-1/4 T. [[cumin|Ground Cumin]], 1-1/4 T. [[oregano|Oregano]], 1 T. salt, 1/2 T. black pepper, chicken] Cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes and up to 12 hours.
# Prepare the Tiborang frying pan along with olive oil on medium heat for grilling.
# Grill the chicken over medium heat with covered lid until cooked through, 6 to 7 minutes per side, depending on the thickness. Let the chicken meat cool down after cook.
# Transfer the cooked chicken to a cutting board and thinly slice. Arrange the chicken on a platter. Arrange naan, pita, or tortillas on another platter. Set out the mint-yogurt dressing. [Note: Directions for this seem to be #6, which seem to be an afterthought and lack explicit details as to which ingredients in similar fashion as to #1; Based on arrangement of ingredient list in original recipe, combine 1-1/2 c. [[yogurt|Yogurt]], 1/2 T. cumin, 3 T. mint, 1 T. [[salt|Salt]]or to taste.]
# Instruct everyone at the table to pile several slices of chicken in a tortilla or pita, add some lettuce if desires, spoon some dressing on top, roll into a wrap, and devour.
# While the chicken is cooking, in a smallish bowl, combine all the ingredients. Cover and refrigerate.
