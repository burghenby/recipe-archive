{H1} Corn Muffins

!! Source
[[Quaker|Quaker brand]] yellow Corn Meal container (A variation from the Easy Corn Bread)
---
''Makes'': 12 muffins

!! Ingredients

* 1-1/4 c. [[all-purpose flour|All-Purpose Flour]]
* 3/4 c. QUAKER or PEARL MILLING COMPANY Enriched Corn Meal
* 1/4 c. sugar
* 2 tsp. [[baking powder|Baking Powder]]
* 1/2 tsp. [[salt|Salt]](optional)
* 1 c. skim milk
* 1/4 c. vegetable oil
* 2 [[egg white|Egg White]]s or 1 egg, beaten

!! Directions

# Heat oven to 400°F. Combine dry ingredients. Stir in milk, oil and egg, mixing just until dry ingredients are moistened.
# Pour batter into 12 greased or paper-lined medium muffin cups. Bake 15 to 20 minutes or until light golden brown.
