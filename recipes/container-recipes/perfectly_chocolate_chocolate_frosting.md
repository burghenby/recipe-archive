{H1} Hershey's "Perfectly Chocolate" Chocolate Frosting

!! Source
[[Hershey's|Hershey brand]] Cocoa Tub
---
''Makes'': About 2 cups frosting

!! Ingredients

* 1/2 cup (1 stick) butter or . [[margarine|Margarine]]
* 3 cups [[powdered sugar|Powdered Sugar]]
* 1 tsp. vanilla extract
* 2/3 cup Hershey's Cocoa
* 1/3 cup milk

!! Directions

# Melt butter. Stir in cocoa. Alternately add powdered sugar and milk, beating to spreading consistency. Add small amount of additional milk, if needed. Stir in vanilla.
