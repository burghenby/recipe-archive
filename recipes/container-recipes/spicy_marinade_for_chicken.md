!! Source
[[Old Bay|Old Bay brand]] seasoning tin
---

!! Ingredients

* 1/4 c. oil
* 2 Tbsp. [[lemon juice|Lemon Juice]]
* 1 Tbsp. Old Bay seasoning
* 1 tsp. [[parsley|Parsley]] flakes
* 1/4 tsp. [[ground black pepper|Ground Black Pepper]]

!! Directions

# Combine ingredients in glass dish or plastic self-closing bag. Add one pound boneless chicken breasts. Refrigerate 20-30 minutes. Broil or grill.
