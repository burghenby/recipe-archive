{H1} Shrimp and Tomatoes on Angel Hair

!! Source
[[Good and Gather|Target brand]] angel hair pasta box
---

!! Ingredients

* 1/2 (16-oz.) pkg. Good and Gather angel hair pasta
* 2 Tbsp. olive oil
* 1 (16-oz.) pkg. frozen large raw shrimp, thawed
* 1 small onion, finely chopped
* 1/2 c. white wine, if desired
* 1 (15-oz.) can diced tomatoes, undrained
* 1/2 c. Kalamata olives
* 2 Tbsp. capers
* 1/4 tsp. crushed red pepper

!! Directions

# Cook angel hair according to package directions.
# Meanwhile, heat oil in large skillet on MEDIUM-HIGH heat; add thawed shrimp. Cook, stirring frequently, 4 to 5 minutes or until shrimp is fully cooked. Remove from heat with slotted spoon.
# Add onion to skillet; cook and stir about 3 minutes or until tender.
# Add wine and cook about 2 minutes or until reduced by half. Stir in tomatoes, olives, capers and red pepper. Heat to boiling; reduce heat and simmer 2 minutes.
# Add shrimp back to skillet and heat through. Serve over pasta. Serve with Parmesan cheese, if desired.
