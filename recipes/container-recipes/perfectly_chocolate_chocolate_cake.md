{H1} Hershey's "Perfectly Chocolate" Chocolate Cake

!! Source
[[Hershey's|Hershey brand]] Cocoa Tub
---
''Makes'': 12 servings

!! Ingredients

* 2 cups sugar
* 1 tsp. [[salt|Salt]]
* 2 tsp. vanilla extract
* 1-3/4 cups all-purpose flour
* 2 [[egg|Egg]]s
* 1 cup boiling water
* 3/4 cup Hershey's Cocoa
* 1 cup milk
* 1-1/2 tsp. [[baking powder|Baking Powder]]
* 1-1/2 tsp. [[baking soda|Baking Soda]]
* 1/2 cup vegetable oil
* "Perfectly Chocolate" Chocolate Frosting

!! Directions

# Heat oven to 350°F. Grease and flour two 9-inch round baking pans.
# Combine dry ingredients in large bowl. Add eggs, oil and vanilla; beat on med. speed 2 min. Stirl in boiling water (batter will be thin). Pour into pans.
# Back 30-35 mins or until wooden pick inserted in center comes out clean. Cool 10 min.; remove from pans to wire racks. Cool completely. Frost with "Perfectly Chocolate" Chocolate Frosting.
