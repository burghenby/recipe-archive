!! Toasted Coconut Flakes
To Toast Coconut

# Preheat [[oven|Oven]] to 325°F. Place a thin layer of [[coconut flakes|Coconut Flakes]] on a baking sheet.
# Bake for 5-10 minutes, until golden brown. After a couple of minutes, stir to ensure even toasting.

!! Tinted Coconut Flakes
To Tint Coconut

# Place 1 cup of coconut flakes in a screwtop jar. Combine 1 to 2 drops of [[food coloring|Food Coloring]] in 1 teaspoon of [[water|Water]]; add to coconut.
# Screw lid and shake well until coconut is uniformly colored.

!! Storing Coconut Flakes
To Store Coconut
# Store in a cool, dry place. Refrigerate after opening.
