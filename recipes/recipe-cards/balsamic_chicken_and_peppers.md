!! Balsamic Chicken and Peppers

!! Source
[[Taste of Home|Taste of Home magazine]] magazine recipe card insert by Valerie Moore
---
''Total Time'': 25 minutes
''Makes'': 4 servings

!! Ingredients

* 4 boneless skinless [[chicken breast|Chicken Breast]] halves (4oz.each)
* 1/4 tsp. [[pepper|Ground Black Pepper]]
* 1/8 tsp. [[salt|Salt]]
* 2 Tbsp. [[olive oil|Olive Oil]], divided
* 1 each medium sweet [[yellow|Yellow Pepper]], [[red|Red Pepper]], and [[orange pepper|Orange Pepper]] julienned
* 1 small [[onion|Onion]], sliced
* 2 Tbsp. [[balsamic vinegar|Balsamic Vinegar]]

!! Steps
# Sprinkle chicken with pepper and salt. In a large skillet, cook chicken in 1 Tbsp. oil for 4-6 minutes one each side or until a thermometer reads 170°; drain. Remove chicken and keep warm.
# In the same skillet, saute peppers and onion in remaining oil for 3-4 minutes or until tender. Return chicken to the pan. Add vinegar; heat through.
