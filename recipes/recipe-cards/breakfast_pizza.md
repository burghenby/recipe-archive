!! Breakfast Pizza

!! Source
[[Produce for Kids|Meijer brand]] campaign recipe card, circa 2018
---
''Makes'': 2 servings

!! Ingredients

* 1 (6-inch) WHole Wheat Pita
* 3 T. [[almond butter|Almond Butter]]
* 1 Lil Snappers Gala Apple, sliced
* 1 T. [[sliced almonds|Almond]]
* 1 tsp. Honey

!! Steps

Toast pita. Top with almond butter, apples and almonds. Drizzle with honey and cut into quarters.
