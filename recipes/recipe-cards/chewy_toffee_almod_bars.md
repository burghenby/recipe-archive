!! Chewy Toffee Almond Bars

!! Source
[[Hershey's|Hershey brand]] Kitchens recipe card
---
''Makes'': 24 bars

!! Ingredients

* 1 c (2 sticks) Butter, softened
* 1/2 c. Sugar
* 2 c. [[all-purpose flour|All-Purpose Flour]]
* 1-1/3 c. (8 oz. pkg.) HEATH BITS 'O BRICKLE Toffee Bits
* 3/4 c. [[light corn syrup|Light Corn Syrup]]
* 1 c. [[sliced almonds|Almond]], divided
* 3/4 c. MOUNDS Sweetened Coconut Flakes, divided

!! Steps

# Heat oven to 350°F. Grease sides of 13x9x2-inch baking pan.
# Beat butter and sugar until fluffy. Gradually add flour, beating until well blended. Press dough into prepared pan.
# Bake 15 to 20 minutes or unti edges are lightly browned. Meanwhile, combine toffee bits and corn syrup in medium saucepan./
Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until toffee is melted (about 10 to 12 minutes). Stir in 1/2 cup almonds and 1/2 cup coconut./
Spread toffee ixture to within 1/4-inch of edges of crust. Sprinkle 1/2 cup almonds and remaining 1/4 cup coconut over top.
# Bake an additional 15 minutes or until bubbly. Cool completely in pan on wire rack. Cut into bars.
