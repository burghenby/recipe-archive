!! Fudgey Special Dark Brownies

!! Source
[[Hershey's|Hershey brand]] Kitchens recipe card
---
''Makes'': About 24 brownies

!! Ingredients

* 3/4 c. HERSHEY'S Cocoa
* 1/2 tsp. [[baking soda|Baking Soda]]
* 2/3 c. Butter or . [[margarine|Margarine]], melted and divided
* 1/2 c. Boiling Water
* 2 c. Sugar
* 2 Eggs
* 1-1/3 c. [[all-purpose flour|All-Purpose Flour]]
* 1 tsp. Vanilla Extract
* 1/4 tsp. [[salt|Salt]]
* 1 c. HERSHEY'S SPECIAL DARK Chocolate Chips

!! Steps

# Heat oven to 350°F. Grease 13x9x2-inch baking pan.
# Stir together cocoa and baking soda in large bowl; stir in 1/3 cup butter. Add boiling water; stir until mixture thickens./
Stir in sugar, eggs and remaining 1/3 cup butter; stir unti smooth. Add flour, vanilla and salt; blend completely. Stir in chocolate chips. Pour into prepared pan.
# Bake 35 to 40 minutes or until brownies begin to pull away from sides of pan. Cool completely in pan on wire rack. Frost if desired./
Cut into squares.
