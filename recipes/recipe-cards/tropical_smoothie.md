!! Tropical Smoothie

!! Source
[[Produce for Kids|Meijer brand]] campaign recipe card, circa 2018
---
''Makes'': 2 servings

!! Ingredients

* 1 c. Frozen Peaches
* 1 tsp. Vanilla Extract
* 1 c. 1% Milk
* 1/2 Banana
* 2 T. Honey
* Ice (if desired)

!! Steps

Place peaches, vanilla, milk, banana and honey in blender and blend until smooth. Add ice as desired for thicker smoothie.
