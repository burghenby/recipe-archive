!! Ingredients

* Bread
* 3 Tbsp. Caviar paste
* 1 tsp. [[lemon juice|Lemon Juice]]
* 1/2 tsp. [[paprika|Paprika]]
* 2 Tbsp. butter
* 1/2 c. Watercress, finely chopped

!! Directions

Make toast rounds, spread with the following mixture; three tablespoonfuls of caviare paste, one teaspoonful of lemon juice, one-half teaspoonful of paprika, two tablespoonfuls of butter, one-half cupful of finely chopped watercress.
