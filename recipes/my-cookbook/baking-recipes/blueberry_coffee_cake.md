{H1} Blueberry Coffee Cake

!! Source
Via Pinterest, original from [Spaceships and Laserbeams](https://spaceshipsandlaserbeams.com/blueberry-coffee-cake/), circa 2019; I've since made my own adjustments, plus the site shows that the original recipe was last updated March 27th, 2025
---

!! Ingredients

* 1/4 c. Butter
* 3/4 c. Sugar + 2 T. set aside
* 1 Egg
* 1 tsp. Vanilla
* 1-1/2 c. Blueberries
* 1-1/2 c. Flour + 1 T. set aside
* 3 tsp. [[baking powder|Baking Powder]]
* 1/2 c. Milk
* 3 T. [[brown sugar|Brown Sugar]]
* 1 tsp. Cinnamon
* 1 tsp. [[ground ginger|Ground Ginger]]
* 1/2 tsp. [[ground nutmeg|Ground Nutmeg]]
* 1/4 tsp. [[ground cloves|Ground Cloves]]
* 1/4 tsp. [[Allspice|Ground Allspice]]

!! Directions

# Preheat oven to 350°F. Cream butter with 3/4 c. sugar. Add egg then vanilla, mixing well after each addition.
# Add flour and baking powder, alternating with milk. Spread half of mixture a in greased 8x8-in. pan.
# Combine blueberries with 2 T. sugar and 1 T. flour. Spread evenly over batter in pan.
# Carefully spread remaining batter over blueberries. In a small bowl, combine brown sugar, cinnamon, ginger, clove, allspice and nutmeg. Sprinkle brown sugar mixture over top.
# Bake for 45-50 minutes or until wooden skewer inserted into center comes out clean. Take care as pierced or burst blueberries can give a false indication of underdone batter.
