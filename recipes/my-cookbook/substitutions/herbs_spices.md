{H1} Herb and Spice Substitutions
---
Allspice, 1 tsp.			.	.	.	.	1/2 tsp. [[ground cinnamon|Ground Cinnamon]] + 1/2 tsp. [[ground cloves|Ground Cloves]]
Anise, 1 tsp.				.	.	.	.	1/2 tsp. dill leaf + 1/2 tsp. [[cumin|Ground Cumin]]
Basil (dry), 1 tsp.			.	.	.	.	1/2 tsp. [[oregano|Oregano]] + 1/2 tsp. [[thyme|Thyme]]
Bay leaf (whole), 1 leaf	.	.	.	.	1/4 tsp. crushed bay leaf
Caraway seeds			.	.	.	.	use anise or fennel seeds
Cardamom, 1 tsp.		.	.	.	.	1/2 tsp. cinnamon + 1/2 tsp. [[ground cloves|Ground Cloves]]
Cayenne pepper, 1 tsp.	.	.	.	.	1/2 tsp. [[paprika|Paprika]] + 1/2 tsp. [[chili powder|Chili Powder]]
Garlic salt, 1 tsp.			.	.	.	.	1/8 tsp. [[garlic powder|Garlic Powder]] + 7/8 tsp. [[salt|Salt]]
Garlic, 1 small clove		.	.	.	.	1/8 tsp. [[garlic powder|Garlic Powder]]
Ginger (raw), 1 Tbsp.		.	.	.	.	1/8 tsp. powdered ginger
Mustard (dry), 1 tsp.		.	.	.	.	1 Tbsp. prepared (bottled) mustard
Onion powder, 1 tsp.		.	.	.	.	1 medium onion (chopped)
						.	.	.	OR	1 Tbsp. dried minced onion
Parsley (dry), 1 tsp.		.	.	.	.	1 Tbsp. fresh parsley (chopped)
Pepper (hot), 1 small		.	.	.	.	1/4 tsp. [[cayenne pepper|Ground Cayenne Pepper]]
						.	.	.	OR	1/4 tsp. Tabasco sauce
Pepper (mild), 1			.	.	.	.	1 Tbsp. [[chili powder|Chili Powder]]
Vanilla bean, 1			.	.	.	.	1 tsp. vanilla extract
Poultry seasoning, 1 tsp.	.	.	.	.	1/4 tsp. [[thyme|Thyme]] + 3/4 tsp. sage
Lemon (medium), 1		.	.	.	.	3 Tbsp. [[lemon juice|Lemon Juice]] (concentrate)
Lime (medium), 1			.	.	.	.	2 Tbsp. [[lime juice|Lime Juice]] (concentrate)
