!! Combination Ideas

# 1 lb pork, 1 tsp. chili powder, 1-1/2 tsp. cumin, 1/2 tsp. paprika, onion, minced garlic
# lamb, oregano, red pepper, cumin, cinnamon, onion, minced garlic, parsley, [[lemon zest|Lemon Zest]]
# protein of choice, oregano, parmesan, onion, minced garlic, rosemary, [[lemon zest|Lemon Zest]]
# chicken, soy sauce, red pepper flakes, toasted sesame seeds, minced garlic, [[lime zest|Lime Zest]]

!! Directions

# Ensure protein is ground up if not already. Preheat oven to 400 degrees F. Mix together chosen ingredients in a large bowl until mixture is uniform.
# Form meatballs so that they are 1-1/2 to 3 oz. in size, depending on your preference. Lay them in a single layer on a baking sheet.
# Bake for 17 minutes or until the internal temperature of the meatballs reaches 165 degrees F.
