{H1} Chili Spice Mix
---
!! ingredients

* 3 Tbsp. [[chili powder|Chili Powder]]
* 2 tsp. [[Cumin|Ground Cumin]]
* 1 1/2 tsp. [[garlic powder|Garlic Powder]]
* 1 1/2 tsp. [[paprika|Paprika]]
* 1 tsp. [[smoke paprika|Smoked Paprika]]
* 1 tsp. [[oregano|Oregano]]
* 1 tsp. [[unsweetened cocoa powder|Unsweetened Cocoa Powder]]
* 1/2 tsp. [[onion powder|Onion Powder]]
* 1/2 tsp. [[salt|Salt]]
* 1/4 tsp. [[thyme|Thyme]]
* 1/4 tsp. [[cayenne pepper|Ground Cayenne Pepper]] powder

!! Uses
This is used exclusively when I make both my stovetop and crockpot chili as well as my all-meat chili spread. I also use it in any recipe that calls for chili seasoning (not specific chili powder) and in any boxed meal that already has chili seasoning but just needs some help.
