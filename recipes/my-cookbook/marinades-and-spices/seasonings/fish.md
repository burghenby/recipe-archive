{H1} Fish Seasoning
---
!! Ingredients

* 1 Tbsp. [[dry basil|Basil]]
* 1 Tbsp. crushed rosemary
* 1 Tbsp. [[dry parsley|Parsley]]
* 2 tsp. [[salt|Salt]]
* 2 tsp. [[ground black pepper|Ground Black Pepper]]
* 2 tsp. [[dry thyme|Thyme]]
* 2 tsp. [[dry marjoram|Marjoram]]
* 1 tsp. [[dry oregano|Oregano]]
* 1 tsp celary salt
* 1 tsp garlic powder

!! Uses

A general-purpose fish seasoning, very good for oven-baked fish.
