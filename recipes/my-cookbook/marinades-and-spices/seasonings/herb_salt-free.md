{H1} Salt-free Herb Seasoning
---
!! Ingredients

* 3 tsp. [[onion powder|Onion Powder]]
* 2 1/2 tsp. [[garlic powder|Garlic Powder]]
* 1 1/2 tsp. [[dried parsley|Dry Parsley]]
* 1 1/4 tsp. [[smoke paprika|Smoked Paprika]]
* 1 tsp. [[sweet paprika|Sweet Paprika]]
* 1 tsp. [[dry mustard|Dry Ground Mustard]]
* 1/2 tsp. dried thyme
* 1/2 tsp. [[dried parsley|Dry Parsley]]
* 1/4 tsp. [[cayenne pepper|Ground Cayenne Pepper]]
* 1/4 tsp. celery seed
* 1/4 tsp. black pepper

!! Uses

Anywhere that I was extra flavor without adding sodium.

!! Notes

The paprika can be substituted for any kind that you have available or that you prefer as long as the total amount equals 2-1/4 tsp.
