{H1} Greek-style Spice Blend
---
!! Ingredients

* 2 Tbsp. [[garlic powder|Garlic Powder]]
* 2 Tbsp. dried oregano
* 1 Tbsp. [[onion powder|Onion Powder]]
* 1/2 Tbsp. [[dried dill|Dry Dill]]
* 1/2 Tbsp. [[salt|Salt]]
* 1/2 Tbsp. black pepper
* 1/2 Tbsp. [[dried parsley|Dry Parsley]]
* 1/2 Tbsp. dried rosemary
* 3/4 tsp. dried thyme
* 1/3 tsp. [[ground nutmeg|Ground Nutmeg]]

!! Uses

Primarily only used to season tuna salad for my tuna salad sandwiches.
