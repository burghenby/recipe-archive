{H1} Montreal Steak
---
!! Ingredients

* 2 Tbsp. [[paprika|Paprika]]
* 2 Tbsp. [[ground black pepper|Ground Black Pepper]]
* 2 Tbsp. . [[kosher salt|Kosher Salt]] (or other [[coarse salt|Coarse Salt]]; if unavailable, [[table salt|Table Salt]] will do)
* 1 Tbsp. [[garlic powder|Garlic Powder]]
* 1 Tbsp. [[onion powder|Onion Powder]]
* 1 Tbsp. [[ground coriander|Ground Coriander]]
* 1 Tbsp. dry dill
* 1 Tbsp. red pepper flakes

!! Uses

Works great rubbed on meat before being seared, excellent in burgers, and is really good on chicken.
