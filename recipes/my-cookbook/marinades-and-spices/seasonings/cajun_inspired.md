{H1} Cajun-Inspired Spice Blend
---
!! Ingredients

* 2 Tbsp. [[paprika|Paprika]]
* 2 Tbsp. [[salt|Salt]]
* 1 1/2 Tbsp. [[garlic powder|Garlic Powder]]
* 1 1/2 Tbsp. dried oregano
* 1 Tbsp. black pepper
* 1 Tbsp. [[ground cumin|Ground Cumin]]
* 1 Tbsp. coriander powder
* 1/2 Tbsp. [[onion powder|Onion Powder]]
* 1/2 Tbsp. dried thyme
* 1/2 Tbsp. [[cayenne pepper|Ground Cayenne Pepper]]
* 1/2 Tbsp. [[smoke paprika|Smoked Paprika]]

!! Uses

I use this for both fish and chicken. It also works well as a rub and as part of a breading mixture.
