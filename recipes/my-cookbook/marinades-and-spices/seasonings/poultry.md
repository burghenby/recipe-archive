{H1} Poultry Seasoning
---
!! Ingredients

* 4 Tbsp. [[ground sage|Ground Sage]]
* 3 tsp. [[ground thyme|Ground Thyme]]
* 2 tsp. [[dry marjoram|Marjoram]]
* 1-1/2 tsp ground rosemary
* 1 tsp. [[ground nutmeg|Ground Nutmeg]]
* 1 tsp. [[ground black pepper|Ground Black Pepper]]

!! Uses

A general use poultry seasoning, generally rubbed over chicken before baking.
